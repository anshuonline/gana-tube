Why GanaTube

Free Online Music Streaming & Social Audio Platform

GanaTube is a premier free music streaming platform built for Indian and international music lovers. Discover and stream millions of songs, trending Hindi tracks, latest Punjabi chartbusters, classic Bollywood melodies, vibrant Bhojpuri tunes, and rich regional melodies across Bengali, Haryanvi, Tamil, Telugu, and English — all in one seamless place. Every track streams in clean, high-definition (HD) audio quality with PC & desktop background playback support, real-time synchronized lyrics, and zero mandatory signups or subscriptions.

Whether you want to explore top hits on our Discovery Charts, enjoy hand-crafted Curated Playlists, jam with friends in synchronized Listen Together Rooms, or browse music by your favorite languages — Hindi, Punjabi, Bhojpuri, English, Haryanvi, Bengali, or Tamil — GanaTube provides a lightning-fast, AMOLED dark, distraction-free audio experience across all devices. Search effortlessly across songs, albums, and artists with smart voice search, save your personalized favorites, and enjoy continuous music wherever you go.
